About Us

MEP execution with practical site focus.

True North Engineering works on mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection systems for commercial and industrial projects in Karachi and nearby areas.

Our Work

Built around coordination, clear scope and dependable follow-through.

We support clients through MEP installation and project execution, including HVAC systems, piping, electrical distribution, plumbing and fire protection work.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the site, define the scope clearly, coordinate with other trades, and keep the client informed as work moves forward.

We do not believe a company profile should be louder than the work itself. The aim is to deliver systems that are installed properly, documented clearly and ready for use.

What We Handle

  • Mechanical and HVAC installation coordination
  • Electrical distribution and site services
  • Plumbing, drainage and fire protection systems
  • Industrial piping and equipment support work
  • Testing, commissioning and handover support
