Our Story

A commitment built on experience, integrity and performance.

True North Engineering was formed to deliver MEP engineering and project execution with clear communication, practical coordination and reliable site follow-through.

We work with clients, consultants and contractors to execute mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection scopes that are safe, coordinated and built to last.
