About Us

MEP execution with practical site focus.

True North Engineering works on mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection systems for commercial and industrial projects in Karachi and nearby areas.

Our Story

A commitment built on experience, integrity and performance.

True North Engineering was formed to deliver MEP engineering and project execution with clear communication, practical coordination and reliable site follow-through.

We work with clients, consultants and contractors to execute mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection scopes that are safe, coordinated and built to last.

Our approach is simple: understand the need, engineer the right solution, execute with precision and support the project through handover.

Founder & Engineering Lead

Muhammad Kamran Rao

Mechanical Engineer | ARP-E

A hands-on Mechanical Engineer with extensive experience across steel manufacturing, industrial operations, maintenance & reliability, project execution, and mechanical utilities.

He has managed an industrial project portfolio exceeding PKR 300 million, along with the delivery of PKR 200+ million in mechanical utilities and HVAC projects - from planning and site execution through commissioning and final client handover.

His professional experience spans Pakistan and Australia, with expertise in cold rolling operations, condition monitoring, root-cause analysis, HVAC systems, piping, mechanical utilities, and project commissioning.

As an Asset Reliability Practitioner Engineer (ARP-E) certified by the Mobius Institute, he brings a reliability-driven approach to engineering and project decisions, combining practical site experience with structured problem-solving, condition-based maintenance, and asset performance principles.
